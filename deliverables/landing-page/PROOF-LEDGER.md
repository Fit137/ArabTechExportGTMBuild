# Proof ledger, Track B landing page

Every number on the page, with its source, tier, and clearance against
`GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 5. Nothing appears in the copy that is not in this table.

**Tier key.** **A** primary source, cite it. **B** credible secondary, describe as
commonly reported. **C** aggregator or vendor content, use direction not figure.

**Clearance key.** **Cleared** describes the buyer's world, says nothing about what a
member earns. **Flagged** sits close to a Section 5 ban, your call. **Refused** not
used.

## Skill adaptation notes

The `landing-page-copywriter` skill is written for SaaS. Six deviations, each
deliberate.

| Skill expects | What was done | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Section 2 "What your [X] desperately needs", three need categories | Replaced with **Why This, Why Now, Why You**, four numbered claims each carrying figures | You asked for the hypothesis spine. Abstract "needs" prose would have buried it |
| Pain sections as prose paragraphs | Each pain led by a number, capped at two short paragraphs | Density request. Every pain now has a figure attached |
| Pricing table with tiers and dollar amounts | Tier shape with `{TBD}` prices | Pricing deferred, `00-ASSUMPTIONS.md` decision 3. The shape is decided, the numbers are not |
| Section 12 social proof, or skip silently | Replaced with **"What We Do Not Have Yet"** | The skill says skip when absent. For a pre-launch business teaching buyers to demand attributable proof, stating the absence is stronger than an unexplained gap. Conflict 5 |
| Product capability bullets in How It Works | Method and dimension bullets | It is a service, not software. There is no UI to screenshot, so `[IMAGE:]` placeholders were dropped |
| ROI math sub-table, "one outcome pays for N years" | Omitted entirely | It is an income claim with arithmetic attached. Dropped from Phase 9 for the same reason |
