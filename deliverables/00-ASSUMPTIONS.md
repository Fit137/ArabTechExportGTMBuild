`GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 4 is four blank lines and five unanswered open decisions.
Every one of them gates a phase. Rather than stop, this run proceeds on stated
assumptions and marks each output that depends on one.

Read this first. Change an assumption here and you know exactly which files to
rebuild.

An assumption is a stated choice about direction. It is never permission to
invent a figure.

## The five open decisions

### 1. Language, Arabic or English, per channel

**Assumed: Arabic for top of funnel, English for the work product.**

Top of funnel organic content, the scorecard, and the email sequence run in
Arabic. Course delivery, community discussion, and every artifact a member will
put in front of a North American buyer run in English or are produced in English.

The reasoning is that the buyer being reached is Arabic-first and the buyer being
sold to is not. A member who cannot operate in English on a sales call has a
problem this business cannot solve, and pretending otherwise in the marketing sets
up a refund. Running the work product in English also makes the community room
double as practice.

**Confidence: medium.** This is the brief's own highest-impact open item and it is
the assumption most likely to be wrong.

**Depends on it:** Phase 6, all 27 headlines. Phase 8, every asset. The lead
magnet. Phase 7 stages 1 and 2.

### 2. The boundary of "Arab"

**Assumed, five named launch markets: Egypt, Jordan, Morocco, Saudi Arabia, and
the United Arab Emirates.** Diaspora included where they follow Arabic content.

The brief asked for three to five named markets rather than a definition, and
these five carry the developer population, the Arabic content consumption, and the
existing government export scaffolding the research found. Tunisia, Algeria,
Lebanon and the rest of the Gulf are second wave rather than excluded.

**Confidence: medium.** Defensible, but it is a founder's decision about who they
are for.

**Depends on it:** Phase 4 ICP location. Phase 5 channels. Phase 7 payment rails,
which differ sharply across these five. Phase 9 regional pricing.

Note the split inside the assumed set. Egypt, Jordan and Morocco are the ones with
the card and currency constraints documented in `00-RESEARCH-FINDINGS.md`. Saudi
Arabia and the UAE are not. One list of five markets, two completely different
payment and pricing realities.

### 3. Pricing decided or deferred

**Assumed: deferred.** Every price in Phase 9 is `{TBD}`.

This is the brief's own documented default and it is the right one. The tier
shape, the value metric, the expansion levers, and the ROI structure are all built
and all usable. Only the numbers are missing, and inventing them would produce a
price with no evidence behind it.

**Confidence: high.** Deferring costs almost nothing here.

**Depends on it:** Phase 9 entirely. Phase 7 stage 4.

### 4. Which track launches first

**Assumed: Track B first**, per the brief's own recommendation.

**Confidence: high.**

Both tracks are built in this run. Track B is the complete one, Track A is built
to the same phase structure and reads across from Track B in Phases 3, 6 and 7
where the brief says the structure carries.

### 5. Founder's stateable background and credentials

**Assumed: nothing stateable yet.** Every Supporting Proof Point and every Social
Proof headline is `{TBD}`.

This is not a guess dressed as an assumption, it is a refusal to fill eighteen
fields the template asks for and the business cannot answer. Phase 6 uses method
and process as the substitute for the social proof headline, exactly as
`SKILL-ADAPTATION.md` directs.

**Confidence: high as a method, low as a fact.** The founder almost certainly has
something stateable. Nobody starts a business teaching market access with nothing.
But it was not in the briefing, and it cannot be written for them.

**Depends on it:** Phase 6, nine copy drills times two fields. Phase 3 statement 1.
The whole of Phase 5 Step 5.2, since a founding cohort is recruited on the founder's
credibility before anything else exists.

**This is the highest-value unblock in the entire run.** One paragraph from the
founder about what they have actually done fills eighteen fields that are
currently empty and turns Phase 6 from a template into copy.

### 5a. Market sequence, United States then Canada

**Assumed, and now explicitly a founder decision rather than a finding.** Conflict 4
in `00-RESOLUTIONS.md`, resolved as option A.

`GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 1 says United States first, then Canada. That stays. What is
removed is the unstated assumption that Canada is the United States with less
competition. Three caveats from `00-BUYER-CHECKS.md` now travel with any Canada
content:

- A federal **Buy Canadian** policy reportedly grants Canadian suppliers a 10 percent
  bid price reduction and 25 percent evaluation-score weight. Public procurement only,
  so it touches few Track B buyers, but it is a structural disadvantage where it applies.
- **Quebec French obligations are statutory**, described in the source as an
  engineering commitment rather than translation.
- Canadian buyers scrutinise **United States** vendors over CLOUD Act exposure. A MENA
  vendor does not carry that risk, which is a small genuine opening and the only
  finding in this run where being foreign helps.

**Confidence: medium.** Enough to remove the easy assumption, not enough to reorder
the markets.

## Two assumptions the research forced, which the brief did not anticipate

### 6. Payment rails are a packaging input, not a checkout detail

`00-RESEARCH-FINDINGS.md` documents monthly USD card caps reported as low as $50
to $200 in Egypt, with international limits suspended on debit cards.

**Assumed: a card charged monthly in USD cannot be the only way to pay.** Phase 9
carries annual and cohort billing, local collection, and a non card route as
structural requirements rather than as options.

**Confidence: high on the constraint, medium on the response.** The constraint is
evidenced. Which alternative rail to build first is a decision this run cannot
make.

### 7. The free content competitor is fragmented, not concentrated

No single dominant named Arabic creator in this niche could be verified.

**Assumed: nobody owns this category's attention.** Phase 2 slot 2 is a category
rather than a company, and Phase 8 is built to compete against volume rather than
against a brand.

**Confidence: medium.** One research pass is weak evidence, and the founder's own
audience will answer this in twenty minutes.
