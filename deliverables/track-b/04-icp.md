## Which one to win first

**Level 2, and this is a change of emphasis worth stating plainly.**

The skill's instinct, and most GTM instinct, is to start at Level 1 because it is
the larger and more visible group. Three findings in this run point the other way.

1. **Level 2 has a live deal.** Phase 1 put both quadrant 1 services on work applied
   to the member's own deal. A Level 1 member with no live deal cannot use the
   differentiated half of the offer, only the commodity half.
2. **Level 2 survives the first fortnight.** The strongest quantified finding in the
   research is 30 to 50 percent churn in the first 14 days among members who never
   act and never post. A member with an active target market deal has something to
   post about on day two. A member without one does not.
3. **Level 2 can pay.** Level 1's income band is unknown and the payment rail
   constraints in `00-RESEARCH-FINDINGS.md` bite hardest at the low end. Someone
   already receiving target market income has a working rail and a reference point
   for the price.

Level 1 is still the larger audience and the correct target for the top of funnel.
The scorecard, the organic content, and the buyer's checklist all speak to Level 1.
The paid room is built for Level 2 first.

That split is deliberate: acquire broad, admit narrow, and let the scorecard do the
sorting.

## Payment rail split inside the ICP

Not a field the skill asks for. It earns a row because it cuts the ICP in half
along a line that has nothing to do with intent.

| Market | Rail reality for a recurring USD charge |
|---|---|
| Egypt | Constrained. Monthly USD card caps reported as low as $50 to $200, international limits reported suspended on debit cards. A monthly USD subscription is unreliable |
| Jordan | `{TBD}`. Not researched. Do not assume it matches Egypt or the Gulf |
| Morocco | Partly constrained. PayPal withdrawal in dirhams via Cash Plus since September 2025 |
| Saudi Arabia | Unconstrained for this purpose |
| United Arab Emirates | Unconstrained for this purpose |

Two of the five named markets can pay a monthly card charge without friction, two
cannot reliably, and one is unresearched. This goes to Phase 9 as a packaging
requirement and to Phase 7 stage 4 as a blocker.
