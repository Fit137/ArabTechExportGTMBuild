## Statement 1. Against direct competitors, paid Arabic technical education

**Against Hsoub Academy, Arabic course platforms, and general freelancing courses:**

Not another course teaching Arab builders a skill they already have. Arab Tech
Export starts after the skill, at the point where a capable builder is invisible to
a buyer in the United States or Canada. The instruction is about one buyer in one
place, and the work happens on the member's own live deal rather than on a
curriculum.

**Why this form.** It concedes the craft ground to Hsoub, which scored 9.1 to this
business's 3.2 on breadth of instruction, and takes the ground nobody holds. It
also refuses the readiness framing: the builder is capable, the buyer just cannot
see them.

---

## Statement 2. Against indirect competitors, marketplaces and free content

**Against Upwork, Mostaql, and the free content library:**

Not a shortcut to more of the same work. A marketplace gives access and takes the
price with it, because a buyer choosing from a list compares on price. Free content
teaches selling in general, made mostly by people selling to a buyer they already
had. Arab Tech Export is about one route, from Arab builders to buyers in the United
States and Canada, and what that specific buyer checks before awarding work.

**Why this form.** It names the real dynamic on marketplaces, list based comparison
drives price down, without claiming anyone will earn more. It also handles the
fragmented free content finding: the criticism is not quality, it is that the
advice was written for a different starting position.

---

## Statement 3. Against the status quo

The status quo for this buyer, per `SKILL-ADAPTATION.md`: keep learning from free
content and keep bidding on marketplaces.

**Against doing nothing differently:**

Not a question of working harder at the same two things. Watching more free content
and sending more marketplace bids are both improvements to a position that was
never the constraint. The capability is there. What is missing is knowing what a
buyer in the United States or Canada checks, and being able to show it. Arab Tech
Export teaches that, and applies it to the member's own live deal.

**Why this form.** The status quo is genuinely good and free, and a statement that
pretends otherwise loses an audience that is using it right now and finding it
useful. The wedge is that free content and marketplace bidding both improve
execution inside a position, and this is about the position.
