# Track B, Phase 8. Assets and Collaterals Library

Step 8.1. Three channels substituted per `SKILL-ADAPTATION.md`. The skill's
defaults, LinkedIn outreach through HeyReach, cold email through Instantly, and
LinkedIn ads, are wrong for this buyer. Cold email to individuals from a business
teaching market access is a bad look, and paid ads against an unproven offer with
no conversion data buys expensive evidence.

Channels used: founder led organic short form, long form depth, and email.

Contributors assume a founder-only team per `00-ASSUMPTIONS.md`. Every row that
says Founder is a row that competes with every other row for the same hours, which
is the real constraint on this phase.

## Sequencing, and why it matters more than the list

A founder-only team cannot build 16 assets at once. Order by what unblocks the most.

| Order | Asset | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Founder background page | Unblocks funnel stage 3 entirely. One day of work. Nothing else in this table matters if a visitor cannot evaluate the founder |
| 2 | The buyer's checklist | Feeds four other assets. Free, no gate, fastest to make |
| 3 | Buyer evaluation walkthrough | The flagship. High effort once, low effort after. Phase 1's build order finding says build the amortising asset before the repeating one |
| 4 | Buyer check series, six short posts | Cut from the walkthrough. Near zero marginal cost once 3 exists |
| 5 | Scorecard plus result email plus sequence | The conversion engine. Needs 2 and 3 to exist to be credible |
| 6 | Terms and cancellation page | Needed before any money is taken. Legal review has a lead time, start it early |
| 7 | Founding cohort invitation | Needs 1 through 6 to exist to be answerable |
| 8 | Everything else | After the founding cohort is running and there is something real to say |

**If runway is short,** and `00-ASSUMPTIONS.md` leaves it `{TBD}`, cut items 3 and 4
and go straight from 2 to 5 to 7. The walkthrough is the best asset in this plan and
it is also the slowest. A founding cohort recruited on the founder's credibility
alone, with the checklist as the only public artifact, is a viable smaller plan.
