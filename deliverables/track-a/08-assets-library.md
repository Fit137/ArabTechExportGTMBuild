# Track A, Phase 8. Assets and Collaterals Library

Step 8.1. Channels substituted per `SKILL-ADAPTATION.md`: founder led professional
network, partnerships, and direct outreach. Closer to the skill's defaults than
Track B, and direct outreach is defensible for enterprise.

**Partnerships get the most attention** because Phase 2 scored credibility at 1.8
out of 10 and Phase 7 identified that score as the blocker at stage 3. A credible
institutional partner substitutes for a track record in a way no content asset can.

Contributors assume a founder-only team. Phase 1 capped concurrent Track A
conversations at roughly four to six for one person, which is the real constraint on
everything below.

## Channel 3. Direct outreach

Defensible for enterprise, and note the contrast with Track B where it was cut. The
difference is that a company with a target market motion has a business reason to
take the conversation, and an individual being cold emailed by a market access
business does not.

| Funnel stage | Asset | Description | Format | Content outline | Specs and QA | Contributors | Inputs required | Timeframe | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU | Target account list | Named companies matching ICP Level 2 in the chosen vertical | Working list | Company, sales function size, evidence of target market activity, named sales leader | Evidence based. A company with no visible target market activity is not Level 2 | Founder | Phase 4 vertical | 1 week | Small and researched. Four to six live conversations is the capacity ceiling |
| TOFU | First contact | Individual, researched, referencing something real about that company | Email or professional network message | One observation about their actual target market activity. One question. No pitch, no attachment | **Individually written.** Volume automation here contradicts the subject being taught and will not survive contact with this buyer | Founder | Target list | Ongoing | Low volume by design. Phase 1's capacity ceiling makes high volume pointless anyway |
| MOFU | Diagnostic offer | The written diagnostic, offered free | Email plus call | What it covers, what it costs, that the sales leader must be present | The sales leader condition is stated at the offer, not at the meeting. Phase 7 stage 3 | Founder | Diagnostic process | 2 days | Stating the condition early disqualifies early, which is a saving at this ticket size |
| BOFU | Scoped proposal | Engagement scope, duration, handover artifact, second engagement pricing | Document | Phase 9 engagement design | Reference permission clause included. Cross border payment route agreed before signature | Founder | Phase 9 | 3 days per proposal | Phase 7 stage 4 blocker, contracting and payment, is handled here or it is handled late |

## Sequencing

| Order | Asset | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Founder background page | Blocks stage 3. One day. Same finding as Track B |
| 2 | Partnership one pager | Everything in Track A accelerates once one institution says yes |
| 3 | Cost of the alternative briefing | Answers the board's preference for a hire. Built entirely from published benchmarks already gathered |
| 4 | Buyer teardown, one vertical | Establishes the destination specific knowledge that is the whole proposition |
| 5 | Diagnostic process and template | Needed before any conversation converts |
| 6 | Handover artifact specification | Needed before any proposal goes out |
| 7 | Target account list, one vertical | After 1 to 6, so outreach lands on something |
| 8 | Readiness self assessment | After a partnership exists to distribute it |

**Do not run Track A and Track B outreach simultaneously with one person.** Phase 1
caps Track A at four to six live conversations, and Track B's founding cohort
recruitment in Phase 5 needs individually written outreach to 8 to 12 people. Both
compete for the same founder hours at the same moment, and `GTM-BRIEF.md` recommends
Track B first for reasons Phase 1 of this track independently supports.
