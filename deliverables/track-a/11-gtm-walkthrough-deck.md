# SLIDE 1, Arab Tech Export GTM Dashboard Suite, Track A

- Nine dashboards built for one buyer, the Arab technology company with an established product entering the United States and Canada
- Category, market entry enablement for AI and technology companies
- Core claim, the question against a hire is not who knows the destination better, it is what remains in the company when that person leaves
- Competitors mapped, Sandler, the hire, outsourced sales agencies, ITIDA Export IT, and the Big Four and MBB firms
- The business is pre-launch, so every metric is {TBD} rather than estimated
- So what, this suite says where the credibility gap is and what closes it

# SLIDE 2, Product Feature Matrix Dashboard

- Substituted for a service business, six vectors instead of two
- Hero service, market entry strategy. Highest demand in the set and no named specialist found running this direction
- Differentiators, sales asset rebuild and pricing against target market comparables. Both score high on differentiation and low on demand, because the buyer does not believe these are the problem until a target market buyer ignores them
- Table stakes, sales team training. Sandler runs more than 250 centres worldwide with an active Dubai franchise. This is the entry line, not the reason to choose
- The economics finding, the first two or three engagements are loss-making in hours because buyer research does not transfer across sub-sectors
- So what, take the second client from the first client's vertical, even at a lower fee

# SLIDE 3, Competitive Market Analysis Dashboard

- Five alternatives, only one of which is a company selling the same thing
- The hire is the real competitor. Median SaaS VP Sales base about $157,500, Series B on target earnings $360,000 to $425,000, about 106 days average time to fill, three to six months ramp, search fees on top
- Agencies win on speed and leave nothing inside the company. Big Four and MBB win on brand and stop before execution inside the client's sales team
- Government export programmes are partners more than rivals. ITIDA Export IT has run 15 rounds and supported more than 370 companies, and its eligibility criteria describe much of our ICP directly
- Evidence is thin by nature. Enterprise buyers do not leave public reviews of consultancies, and this is recorded as thin rather than filled
- So what, the competitor to beat is a job posting, not a company

# SLIDE 5, ICP Dashboard

- Two levels read as buyer maturity. Level 1 established at home with no deliberate motion, Level 2 with early traction and no strategy or trained team
- Win Level 2 first. They arrive convinced, the trigger is recent and specific, and the diagnostic can name the failure in the first conversation
- Level 1 requires establishing that a problem exists, which is a long argument for a business with no track record
- Qualification signal is a sales function that exists, at minimum a lead plus two reps. Below that the training half has nobody to train
- Narrow by vertical before the second engagement, not after the fifth
- So what, the ICP is defined by a stalled deal they can name, not by a revenue band

# SLIDE 6, Buying Committee Dashboard

- Three real personas, unlike Track B. The rep, the sales leader, the founder
- The rep has low influence on the decision and decisive influence on the outcome. An untrained team can attend everything and change nothing
- The sales leader is the critical blocker. A training engagement bought over their head is an implicit judgement on their competence. It does not get refused, it dies quietly in scheduling
- Five rules prevent it, and the first is that the sales leader is in the room before the proposal exists
- First client profile, ICP Level 2, sales leader available, reference permission agreed in writing at the start rather than requested at the end
- So what, a founder who will not put the sales leader in the room is a disqualification, not an obstacle

# SLIDE 7, Customer Journey Funnel Dashboard

- Seven stages, with stage 7 as a follow-on engagement rather than a subscription renewal
- Stage 3 carries the single most predictive KPI in Track A, whether the sales leader attended the diagnostic meeting
- Stage 4 blocker is cross border contracting. Currency of invoice, foreign supplier registration, withholding tax, and in some markets central bank approval before a foreign invoice can be paid at all
- Stage 5 measures live deals worked in sessions, sales leader attendance, and rep application between sessions
- Stage 6 activates the reference that was agreed at stage 4, which is the first entry in a track record that is currently empty
- So what, the payment route is agreed before signature or it is discovered late

# SLIDE 8, Lead Magnet, Market Entry Readiness Diagnostic

- Two artifacts. A short self serve assessment that partners distribute, and a written diagnostic that costs founder hours and does the converting
- The written diagnostic is free, six to ten pages, evidenced from the client's own material rather than from a template
- It must contain at least one thing the client does not need us for. A diagnostic that recommends everything on the price list is a proposal wearing a diagnostic's clothes
- Delivered in a meeting with the sales leader present, because hearing the finding together is what prevents the quiet failure
- Costs 8 to 15 founder hours per prospect, which caps concurrent conversations at four to six
- So what, with no references to offer, the only available proof is the work itself, given before payment

# SLIDE 9, Assets and Collaterals Library Dashboard

- Three channels, founder led professional network, partnerships, and direct outreach
- Partnerships get the most attention because credibility scored 1.8 and a credible institution substitutes for a track record in a way no content asset can
- Named partnership targets, ITIDA Export IT in Egypt, int@j and Jordan Source in Jordan, Flat6Labs across Cairo, Riyadh and Abu Dhabi
- Direct outreach is defensible here and was cut from Track B, because a company with a target market motion has a business reason to take the conversation and an individual does not
- Sequencing, founder background page first, partnership one pager second, cost of the alternative briefing third
- So what, do not run Track A and Track B outreach at the same time with one person. Both need individually written founder hours in the same week

# SLIDE 10, Pricing and Packaging Dashboard

- No tiers. Four subscription plans do not describe a consulting engagement
- Value metric, per engagement with fixed scope and duration. Secondary metric, per market, which makes expansion a scope decision rather than a fresh sale
- Success fees and revenue share were excluded, not scored low. They are an outcome claim with an attribution argument inside them
- The diagnostic is free, the second market price is quoted alongside the first, and the retainer is unavailable until handover is delivered and used
- ROI anchor 2 is the closer, setting the engagement against the published first year cost of a target market sales leader including ramp, time to fill and search fees
- So what, those benchmarks describe what alternatives cost. They are never restated as what a client will achieve
