## Step 4.1. Two level ICP

| Attribute | ICP Level 1. Established at home, no deliberate target market motion | ICP Level 2. Early target market traction, no strategy or trained team |
|---|---|---|
| Segment | Arab technology companies with an established product or service in market | The same, with target market revenue already arriving |
| Sub-industries | B2B SaaS, AI products and platforms, technology services and development firms, systems integrators with a productised offer | The same, weighted to B2B SaaS and AI products where the target market buyer is reachable without a local entity |
| Location | Egypt, Jordan, Morocco, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates. `00-ASSUMPTIONS.md` decision 2 | Same, weighted to Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Jordan, where outbound motion is more common |
| Revenue | `{TBD}`. Qualification signal is a sales team that exists, not a revenue number | `{TBD}`. Signal is target market revenue arriving without a plan behind it |
| Team size | Has an identifiable sales function. At minimum a sales lead plus two reps. Below that, the training half of the offer has nobody to train | Same, usually larger, often with one person informally handling foreign inbound |
| Business maturity | Product is proven at home. Sells successfully in the local market. Has never run a deliberate motion at a foreign buyer | Has closed target market deals, usually inbound, referral, or a conference. Cannot repeat it and cannot forecast it |
| Business model | Local pricing, local contracting, local sales cycle assumptions carried into every conversation | Beginning to hit the mismatch: target market buyers ask questions the local motion never had to answer |
| Best engagement model | Diagnostic first, then a bundled strategy and training engagement | Same engagement, shorter diagnostic, faster to scope. Advisory retainer is realistic here |
| Challenges and pain points | Sales team sells the way it sells at home, which is the only way it knows. No idea what a North American buyer checks about a foreign vendor. | Why they should choose us | Because sales training without a destination trains the team to sell better to the wrong buyer, and a strategy document without training produces a plan nobody executes. | Strategic goals | Open a second market that is not correlated with the home economy. Reduce dependence on local budget cycles. | Triggers | A board or investor asked about international revenue. Lost a local deal to a foreign vendor. Local currency or budget pressure. An inbound foreign enquiry nobody knew how to handle. A government export programme opened a round | Closed one or two target market deals and stalled. A rep who was good at home is failing abroad. Considering a target market hire and hesitating on the cost. A deal died at procurement or security review |

## Which one to win first

**Level 2, decisively.**

Level 2 companies have already discovered that selling into the target market is
different. Level 1 companies have not, and a large part of any Level 1 engagement is
spent establishing that there is a problem at all. With no track record to shorten
that argument, see the 1.8 credibility score in Phase 2, Level 1 is a long and
expensive sale for a business that cannot yet point to anyone it has done this for.

Level 2 arrives already convinced. The trigger is specific and recent, a stalled
deal or a failing rep, and the diagnostic can name what went wrong in the first
conversation.

## Vertical concentration

Phase 1 found that the first two or three engagements are loss-making in hours
because buyer research does not transfer across sub-sectors. The ICP should be
narrowed by vertical before the second engagement, not after the fifth.

| Criterion | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| A target market buyer reachable without a local entity | Otherwise the engagement's first finding is that the client needs a US entity, which is a different business |
| A recognisable buying committee | The Phase 5 personas need to map to real job titles the client will recognise |
| At least three plausible clients in the same vertical in the named markets | Concentration is the only route to sane unit economics |
| Procurement and security review as a known hurdle | This is where Level 2 deals stall, and it is a specific, teachable body of knowledge |

Do not pick the vertical here. Pick it from the first engagement, then take the
second client from the same one even at a lower fee.
