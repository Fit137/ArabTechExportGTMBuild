# Track A, Phase 3. Positioning

Step 3.1. Three statements. Same claim constraints as Track B, `GTM-BRIEF.md`
Section 5.

---

## Statement 1. Against direct competitors, sales training and consultancies

**Against Sandler, Winning by Design, and general market entry consultancies:**

Not a sales methodology that works anywhere. Arab Tech Export teaches one
destination, the United States and Canada, to a company selling from outside it. A
method without a destination produces a team that sells well to a buyer it has
never met. The engagement ends with the capability inside the client's own team.

---

## Statement 2. Against indirect competitors, the hire and the agency

**Against hiring a target market sales leader, and against renting an agency:**

Not a question of who knows the market. A hired sales leader knows it first hand and
an agency has the relationships already. The question is what remains afterwards. A
hire takes the knowledge with them when they leave, and an agency never had it
inside your company at all. This builds the capability where it stays.

**Why this form.** It concedes the strongest point the alternatives have rather than
disputing it, which is both true and more persuasive than a claim of superior market
knowledge that a buyer has no reason to accept from a business with no track record.

---

## Statement 3. Against the status quo

The status quo for this buyer, per `SKILL-ADAPTATION.md`: keep selling at home and
revisit the target market later.

**Against waiting:**

Not a decision between now and later. It is a decision between deliberate and
accidental, because the target market conversations are already happening by
referral, at conferences, and through inbound that nobody planned for. The question
is whether the next one is handled by a team that knows what that buyer checks, or
by the same approach that works at home.

**Why this form.** The buyer is established and successful at home. A statement that
treats waiting as negligence insults a company that is doing fine. The wedge is that
the target market motion already exists in an unmanaged form, which is almost always
true and is easy for a founder to verify against their own inbox.
