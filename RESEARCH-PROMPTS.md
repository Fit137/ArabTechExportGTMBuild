# External research prompts, pre-filled

Three steps in the skill need you rather than the model: Step 1.1, Step 2.1, and Step 2.7. Each is filled with Arab Tech Export's details and ready to paste into Perplexity, Browse AI, or a browser.

There is also a Step 0 below that the skill does not have. You need it, because the skill assumes you already know your five or six competitors by name and this business has not named any yet.

Run Step 0 first. Everything after it depends on the slate it produces.

Prompts below are written for **Run 1, Track B, individual builders.** Track A deltas are at the end.

---

## Step 0. Source the competitor slate

Not in the skill. Do this before Phase 1.

```
I am researching the competitive set for a business called Arab Tech Export.

It teaches Arab software engineers, indie hackers, AI consultants, and AI service
providers how to reach, price to, and be credible to buyers in the United States
and Canada. It delivers this through a paid community, courses, one-on-one
sessions, and AMA sessions.

Find me real, currently operating examples in each of these six categories.
For each, give the name, the website URL, what they charge if public, and who
they serve.

1. A paid community or cohort course teaching technical people to sell to
   Western or United States clients
2. A large free-content creator teaching the same subject, on YouTube, X, or
   LinkedIn
3. A freelance marketplace where this audience currently finds buyers
4. A general freelancing or consulting business course that this audience would
   find first when searching
5. A regional accelerator, incubator, or founder program serving builders in
   Arab-region markets
6. A government or trade export promotion program in an Arab-region market that
   supports technology services exports

For each one, tell me whether it is aimed at the Arab region specifically or is
global. Prioritise ones aimed at this region or with visible users from it.

Do not include anything you cannot verify with a working URL.
```

**Selection rule when the results come back.** Keep the five or six your buyer would actually consider, not the five or six that look most like you. If a free creator has more of your audience's attention than any paid community, that creator is competitor number one and belongs in slot 1, not slot 2.

**Record what you find in a table with these columns:** name, URL, category slot, price if public, who it serves, and whether it is region-specific. Phase 2 reads from this.

## Step 1.1. Feature and service demand research

The skill's version researches software features. This is the services version. Paste after Step 0 is done, with your six names filled in.

3. [name] - [url]
4. [name] - [url]

## Step 2.1. Competitor deep research

```
I need to conduct competitive research for the market access education for AI
builders category, with focus on Arab software engineers, indie hackers, AI
consultants, and AI service providers selling into the United States and Canada.

RESEARCH SCOPE

For each competitor below, gather:
- Website URL
- Tagline from the homepage
- Sub-tagline or secondary headline
- Full description from the homepage and about page
- Strengths, from reviews, testimonials, and public discussion
- Weaknesses, from complaints and negative feedback
- Who they say they serve, in their own words
- What they charge, if public

COMPETITORS TO RESEARCH:
1. [name] - [url]
2. [name] - [url]
3. [name] - [url]
4. [name] - [url]
5. [name] - [url]
6. Arab Tech Export (ours) - arabtechexport.com

SOURCES TO CHECK:
- Company websites: homepage, about, curriculum, pricing pages
- Reddit, X, and YouTube comments
- Course and community platform reviews
- Trustpilot
- LinkedIn company pages
- Public discussion in Arab-region developer and freelancer communities

Note: this category is not reviewed on G2 or Capterra. Those are software review
sites. Do not substitute an unrelated software company that happens to match the
name.

Return findings in a structured format matching the attributes above.
```

**Note on Arab Tech Export's own row.** The skill asks you to research your own product alongside the competitors. The site is not live yet, so fill that row from `GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 1 rather than from a crawl, and mark the strengths and weaknesses rows `{TBD}` rather than writing aspirational ones. A self-assessment written as evidence is the fastest way to get a wrong Phase 2.

## Step 2.7. Review analysis, substituted

The skill sends you to G2. This category is not on G2. Same question, different sources.

```
I need review analysis to define positioning vectors for the market access
education for AI builders category.

RESEARCH SCOPE

For each competitor below, find published buyer feedback and analyse it.

WHAT BUYERS LOVE:
- Extract repeated positive themes
- Identify what each one is seen as leading in
- Count how often each theme appears
- These are what competitors currently lead in

WHAT BUYERS DISLIKE OR WISH FOR:
- Extract repeated complaints
- Identify gaps and unmet wishes
- Count how often each theme appears
- These are the openings

COMPETITORS TO ANALYSE:
1. [name] - [url]
2. [name] - [url]
3. [name] - [url]
4. [name] - [url]
5. [name] - [url]

WHERE TO LOOK, since this category is not on G2 or Capterra:
- Reddit threads discussing the competitor by name
- X threads and quote posts
- YouTube comments under the competitor's own videos
- Course and community platform review sections
- Trustpilot
- Public posts from people who left, which are the most useful and the hardest
  to find
- The competitor's own testimonials, read for what they carefully do not claim

OUTPUT NEEDED:
For each competitor:
- Top 3 positive themes, with frequency
- Top 3 negative themes, with frequency
- Whether the complaints are about content quality, about the community going
  quiet, about access to the founder, or about results not arriving

Return findings in a structured format.
```

**What to watch for.** For paid communities the recurring complaint is almost always the same: the room went quiet, or the promised access to the founder was not there. If that shows up across the slate, it is the single most important finding in Phase 2, because it is the same failure Arab Tech Export will face in month two. Carry it into Phase 7 stage 5 and into Phase 9, since it is what a member is actually paying to avoid.

## Track A deltas

Same three steps, four changes.

1. **Category and niche.** Replace with: market entry enablement for AI and technology companies, focused on Arab technology companies with an established product entering the United States and Canada.

2. **The slate.** Use the Track A slate in `GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 2. Slot 4 is the hire, a target-market sales leader, which has no website. Research it from job postings instead: compensation band, required experience, typical time to hire, and typical tenure. That is its cost and its weakness, and it is the alternative this buyer reaches for first.

3. **Sources.** Enterprise buyers do not leave public reviews of consultancies. Substitute: case studies on the competitor's own site read for what they avoid claiming, LinkedIn posts from named clients, conference talks, and trade body published materials. Expect thin evidence and record it as thin rather than filling the gap.

4. **Our own row.** Same rule as Track B. `{TBD}` rather than aspirational.

## Before you start Phase 1

The skill runs 5 to 6 hours across multiple sessions, one phase per session with review between. These three research steps sit outside that estimate and gate Phases 1 and 2. Doing them properly first is what makes the rest of the run worth anything, and Step 0 is what makes these three possible.
